AI Transparency

Generative AI vs. Deterministic Ranking

In this prototype, Google Gemini is never used to hallucinate or invent movie titles. The language model is strictly confined to interpreting user natural language and normalizing preferences. All ranking and candidate selections are computed deterministically by the Python backend against our local verified dataset.

100-Point Scoring Model:

  • Genre Match (35 pts): Overlap ratio between requested and catalog genres
  • Mood & Tags (25 pts): Emotional vibe and thematic tag alignment
  • Language Match (15 pts): Audio language compatibility
  • Content Type (10 pts): Movie vs. Series alignment
  • Duration Compatibility (10 pts): Graceful penalty for runtimes over preferred limit
  • Quality Rating Boost (5 pts): Critic/audience rating tie-breaker
